# AI SDLC DPF Formation Reference

## Status and purpose

**Status:** historical/reference carrier. This document is informative and does not create DPF, organization or project authority.

It records the declared formation approach and publication boundary of the AI SDLC DPF distributed with the Engineering Work Kit. The current operational route for a new/revised DPF or cheaper non-framework outcome is documented separately in [DPF Formation Method](DPF_FORMATION_METHOD.md).

## Governing basis

AI SDLC DPF was formed from **First Principles Framework — FPF** as a Domain Principles Framework for designing, adapting and improving agentic processes for developing software systems.

FPF supplied governing conceptual distinctions and framework-construction methods. AI SDLC DPF is not a summary of FPF: it specializes those distinctions for a bounded domain. The model, prompts or tools used during preparation do not create authority.

The controlled DPF publication identifies the governing FPF version and hash in its publication-control and governance carriers.

## Formation approach

The declared approach was an LLM-assisted, evidence-grounded, multi-pass framework-engineering process. Material activities included:

- domain and Primary Entity of Concern framing;
- separation of normative, reference, profile and informative content;
- pattern-corpus selection and normalization;
- source and claim mediation;
- case, countercase and comparator analysis;
- traceability and no-shadow review;
- internal and independent review;
- repair cycles and release decision.

This description is historical context, not evidence that every future DPF change has completed the same process. A new controlled change must follow the current governance and preserve its own evidence.

## Historical formation path

```text
governing FPF
→ domain and EoC framing
→ candidate pattern corpus
→ source and applicability work
→ E8 normalization
→ E21 evaluation
→ internal and independent review
→ repair
→ consistency and authority review
→ release decision
```

This sequence describes the historical AI SDLC DPF formation only. It is not the current universal entry route, and it does not make E.21 evaluation, independent review or repair mandatory for every future DPF use/change. Current work first selects its applicable result through `E.4.DPF`; `E.4.PFAD`, `E.4.DPF.DA` and any further review/repair apply only when their own predicate and receiving use are present.

## Distributed-kit boundary

The current `frameworks/specializations/AI_SDLC_DPF/` directory distributes:

- the normative DPF pattern language;
- Reference Architecture;
- Reference Process;
- Primary Application Profile;
- Application Guide;
- glossary, source, traceability, governance and open-question summaries;
- worked examples;
- a combined readable publication view.

The distributed Work Kit does **not** claim to contain the complete development-history corpus, governing FPF text, internal work logs, reviewer conversations or every provenance-heavy source artifact. Where an identity or hash is recorded without the corresponding source file, it is a dependency/provenance reference rather than a bundled authority copy.

## Authority boundary

Historical formation material cannot:

- redefine a normative DPF pattern;
- make reference content normative;
- assign organization or project authority;
- prove regulatory compliance or product assurance;
- authorize automatic modification of `frameworks/specializations/AI_SDLC_DPF/`.

A controlled DPF change remains a separate formal task requiring impact review, verification and an authorized release decision.
